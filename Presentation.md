# Spoken Script - AI Viva System

## Slide 0: PROJECT TITLE

### How to Speak:
Start with a high energy but professional greeting. Stand straight, look at the external examiner. Don't read the slide verbatim.

### What to Speak:
"Good morning everyone. I am here to present our final year project, **SCIRE: An Intelligent AI-Proctored Viva System**. This project addresses the critical need for scalable, authentic oral assessments in modern education. It was designed and implemented by [Name] under the guidance of [Guide Name]."

---

## Slide 1: INTRODUCTION

### How to Speak:
Confident, calibrated pace. Establish the "why" immediately. Use hand gestures to emphasize "shift" from written to oral.

### What to Speak:
"Today, the biggest challenge in education is not *delivering* content, but *authenticating* knowledge. We have moved into an era where written assignments can be generated by AI in seconds. We believe that **oral defense**, or the 'viva voce', is the only true metric of understanding. However, conducting one-on-one reviews for hundreds of students is logistically impossible. **Scire** bridges this gap. It is an autonomous, voice-first system that conducts human-like interviews, allowing us to evaluate students at scale without compromising on the depth of accurate assessment."

---

## Slide 2: SOFTWARE / HARDWARE REQUIREMENTS

### How to Speak:
Brief and technical. Show you know your stack. Don't just list items; explain *why* you chose them.

### What to Speak:
"To build a system this responsive, we needed a robust modern stack. On the client side, we use **Next.js 16** and **React 19**, enhanced with **GSAP** for high-performance animations and **Shadcn UI** for accessibility. For the backend, we operate a monolithic **Service-Oriented Architecture** using **FastAPI**. We use **pgvector** in PostgreSQL to store syllabus embeddings for our RAG interactions, and **Redis** handles our sub-millisecond session state management. We also integrate **Deepgram** for streaming speech-to-text and **ElevenLabs** for natural voice generation, all containerized via Docker for easy deployment."

---

## Slide 3: PROBLEM STATEMENTS

### How to Speak:
Serious tone. You are identifying pain points that the examiners likely face themselves. Nod to acknowledge their struggle.

### What to Speak:
"We identified four critical problems in the current system. First is **Scalability**: A single external examiner simply cannot interview 60 students effectively in a single day; fatigue sets in, and quality drops. Second is **Subjectivity**: One student might get an easy question, another a hard one. Third is the **'ChatGPT Loophole'**: Written code submissions no longer prove a student wrote the code. And finally, the massive **Logistical Overhead** of scheduling venues and panels. We built Scire specifically to eliminate these bottlenecks."

---

## Slide 4: PRELIMINARY DESIGN

### How to Speak:
Visual and architectural. Imagine drawing the flow in the air. Emphasize "Real-time key".

### What to Speak:
"Our design philosophy was 'Latency First '. We didn't build a chatbot; we built a real-time voice pipeline. As you can see in the design, the architecture is event-driven. The client establishes a secure **WebSocket** connection with our Orchestrator. Audio is streamed in binary chunks, processed by the **Voice Activity Detector** to detect when a student stops speaking, and then sent to the LLM. The crucial part here is the **RAG Engine**—it pulls context dynamically from the syllabus, ensuring the AI asks relevant questions, not just random ones."

---

## Slide 5: PROJECT IMPLEMENTATION AND REAL-TIME EXAMPLE

### How to Speak:
Enthusiastic. You are showing off the cool part. Tell a story of a specific interaction.

### What to Speak:
"The 'Exam Loop' is the heart of Scire. In our v2.0 update, we introduced a strict flow. First, the student enters a unique **8-character Exam Code**—this prevents unauthorized access. They acthen complete a mandatory onboarding where we capture an **Identity Snapshot** stored securely in S3. Once the session starts, the AI asks probing questions based on the retrieved syllabus chunks. If a student hesitates, our VAD system detects the silence, and the AI gently nudges them, creating a dynamic, flowing conversation rather than a rigid interrogation."

---

## Slide 6: SECURITY FEATURES

### How to Speak:
Firm and authoritative. Security is non-negotiable. Reassure them that cheating is impossible.

### What to Speak:
"Security is paramount. We implemented a multi-layered protocol starting with **Identity Verification**—capturing a snapshot to match against the student profile. During the exam, we monitor integrity in real-time, tracking tab switches and environmental noise levels. Finally, we solved the 'grade tampering' issue by issuing **Time-Bound Result Tokens**. These are secure, single-use links sent directly to the student's email that expire after 30 days, ensuring that the results you see are authentic and immutable."

---

## Slide 7: FUTURE SCOPE

### How to Speak:
Visionary but grounded. Show you are thinking ahead.

### What to Speak:
"While the current system is robust, we see massive potential for evolution. Our meaningful next step is **Horizontal Scalability** using Kubernetes to support university-wide exams with thousands of concurrent users. We also plan to introduce **Multimodal Capabilities**, allowing students to draw system designs on a digital whiteboard while explaining them. Finally, we aim to integrate directly with LMS platforms like Canvas or Moodle, so grades are automatically pushed to the university database, closing the administrative loop entirely."

---

## Slide 10: THANK YOU

### How to Speak:
Humble, smiling, and open. Invite engagement.

### What to Speak:
"In conclusion, Scire represents the future of assessment—where we value the *student's voice* over their typed text. We have successfully tested the prototype, and the results demonstrate high reliability and fairness. We are now open to your questions and feedback. Thank you."
